2. About the Event

About Tech Fest 2026

Tech Fest 2026 is a college-level technology event designed to encourage students to learn, innovate, and showcase their talents.

The event provides a platform for students to demonstrate their technical knowledge, improve their communication skills, and work together as a team.

Our mission is to inspire young minds to think creatively, solve real-world problems, and explore the latest developments in technology.

Our Goals:

  • Encourage innovation and creativity.

  • Improve technical and problem-solving skills.

  • Promote teamwork and collaboration.

  • Provide opportunities to learn new technologies.
